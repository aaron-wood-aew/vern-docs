Meet Vern.

A private, AI-powered family assistant that lives on your own hardware. Scheduling, meal planning, household coordination, and more — all through natural conversation via iMessage.

What Vern Does

📅

Calendar & Scheduling

Manages family calendars, coordinates schedules, and sends reminders — all through simple text messages.

🍽️

Meal Planning & Recipes

Suggests meals based on what's in your pantry, manages shopping lists, and keeps track of family favorites.

🌤️

Weather & Briefings

Delivers personalized weather forecasts and daily briefings tailored to each family member's schedule.

✅

Chores & Tasks

Assigns and tracks household chores with built-in allowance tracking for kids.

📰

News & Headlines

Curated local and national news delivered in a family-friendly format.

💬

Natural Conversation

Just text Vern like you would a family member. No apps to learn, no interfaces to navigate.
