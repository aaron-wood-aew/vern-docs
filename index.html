Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 18, 2026  |  Last Updated: March 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Vern ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our family assistant messaging service.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Vern via SMS or iMessage, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Your phone number, name, and communication preferences as provided by you or an authorized family administrator.
  • Message Content: The text content of messages you send to and receive from Vern, used solely to provide the requested services (scheduling, reminders, family coordination).
  • Usage Data: Timestamps, message frequency, and interaction patterns to maintain service quality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information exclusively to:

  • Provide family coordination and scheduling services
  • Deliver requested information (weather, calendar, reminders)
  • Authenticate authorized users
  • Improve service reliability and response quality
We do not: sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is never used for advertising.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored locally on privately-owned hardware within the United States. We employ encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews to protect your information. No data is stored on public cloud services.

4. Data Retention

Message content is retained for up to 90 days to provide conversation context and service continuity. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting the family administrator or by texting "DELETE MY DATA" to Vern.

5. Third-Party Services

Vern may interact with the following third-party services to fulfill requests:

  • Twilio: SMS/MMS message delivery (subject to Twilio's Privacy Policy)
  • Apple iMessage: Message delivery for iMessage users
  • Weather services: To provide weather information (no personal data shared)

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information by contacting the family administrator
  • Opt out of messaging at any time by texting STOP

7. Consent to Receive Messages

By providing your phone number to an authorized Vern family administrator, you consent to receive SMS and/or iMessage communications from Vern related to scheduling, reminders, and family coordination. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your interactions.

To opt out: Text STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Text HELP for assistance.

8. Children's Privacy

Vern is a family service that may be used by minors under parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. All minor accounts are created and managed by parent or guardian administrators.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
