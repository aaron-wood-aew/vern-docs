Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 18, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Vern ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our family assistant service through the Vern Companion iOS app, SMS, or other supported channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Vern, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and family role as provided by you or an authorized family administrator during setup.
  • Conversation Content: Messages you send to and receive from Vern through the app or SMS, used solely to provide the requested services (scheduling, reminders, family coordination).
  • Calendar & Email Data: When you connect your Google or Apple account, Vern reads your calendar events and email to coordinate schedules and surface important information for your family.
  • Health & Fitness Data: If you grant access, Vern reads Apple HealthKit data (steps, workouts, activity) to provide personalized fitness insights. Health data is processed on-device and on your home server only.
  • Usage Data: Timestamps, interaction patterns, and feature usage to maintain service quality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information exclusively to:

  • Provide family coordination and scheduling services
  • Deliver requested information (weather, calendar, reminders)
  • Authenticate authorized users
  • Improve service reliability and response quality
We do not: sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is never used for advertising.

3. Data Storage & Security

Vern is a local-first system. All data is stored and processed on your privately-owned home server — not in the cloud. We employ encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit, role-based access controls, and regular security reviews. Sensitive credentials such as API keys are stored in an encrypted vault on your server.

4. Data Retention

Conversation content is retained for up to 90 days to provide context and service continuity. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting the family administrator or through the Vern Companion app settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Vern may interact with the following third-party services to fulfill requests:

  • Google OAuth: Account sign-in for calendar and email access (subject to Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Apple HealthKit: On-device fitness and health data (never sent to external servers)
  • Apple WeatherKit: Weather forecasts and conditions (no personal data shared)
  • Cloud AI Providers (optional): If enabled by the family administrator, Vern may send conversation context to AI providers (Anthropic, Google Gemini, or OpenAI) to enhance responses. This is opt-in and disabled by default.
  • Twilio: SMS/MMS message delivery for text-based interactions (subject to Twilio's Privacy Policy)

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information by contacting the family administrator
  • Opt out of messaging at any time by texting STOP

7. Consent & Communications

By creating an account in the Vern Companion app or being added by an authorized family administrator, you consent to receive communications from Vern related to scheduling, reminders, and family coordination. These communications may be delivered through the Vern app, push notifications, or SMS. Message and data rates may apply for SMS. Message frequency varies based on your interactions.

To opt out of SMS: Text STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages. Text HELP for assistance. Push notifications can be managed in the app or your device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Vern is a family service that may be used by minors under parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. All minor accounts are created and managed by parent or guardian administrators.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
